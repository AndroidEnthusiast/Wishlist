Privacy

Last changed 12 November 2026

Wishlist is an Android app and these web pages. This page says, in plain words, what is kept, where, who can see it, for how long, and how to have it deleted.

On your phone

  • Your wishlists, wishes, photos, private wishes, "Only me" lists, the bin, price history and settings are kept on your phone.
  • Private wishes and "Only me" lists never go online, except inside your locked recovery copy (below), which we can't read.
  • Android may include the app's data in your phone's own backup to your Google account, if you have that switched on in Android's settings.

Online

To share lists, the app and these pages use a database service (Supabase). What is stored there:

  • Wishlists you share (their wishes and pictures) and collab wishlists. Anyone with the link can see a shared list. Lists are never public or searchable.
  • Reservations, linked to a random number for your phone or browser. The person whose list it is never sees who reserved what.
  • Your profile, if you make one: your name, photo, birthday, "I like…", sizes, friend code, friends, and which of your lists friends can see. Only your friends see these.
  • Wishlist groups and gift exchanges you join: your name and the wishlist you joined with, for the other members.
  • Reactions on wishes and "Is it still being bought?" questions.
  • Your recovery copy: a copy of your phone's data, locked on your phone with your recovery key before it is sent. We can't read it, and the key never goes online.

Chats

Messages and photos in chats are locked on the sender's phone and unlocked on the receiver's phone (end-to-end). The server only stores locked messages it can't read. To be honest about the limits: the server hands out the keys that lock messages, so this protection depends on the server being honest. Messages are deleted after a year.

Reports and blocking

If you report someone, we receive your reason, your note, and the last 20 messages of your chat with them (unlocked by your phone, so we can read those). Reports are kept for 12 months. Blocking someone is not reported to anyone, and they are not told.

Other things we keep

  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics, by Google): what went wrong, your phone's model, Android version and app version. No wishes or names.
  • Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging, by Google): an address for your phone, so friends' messages arrive at once.
  • Limits against abuse: a scrambled form of your internet address, kept for one day.
  • Reading a shop's page: when you paste a link in the browser editor, our server loads that page for you. The shop sees our server, not you.
  • How many times a list was opened, without saying by whom.

How long

  • Lists and profiles that the owner's app hasn't opened for 12 months are deleted online. You get a reminder a month before. The copy on your phone is never touched.
  • Recovery copies not updated for 13 months are deleted.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Profile, then "Delete my profile and data" at the bottom. Your profile, friends, chats, reservations and wishlists are deleted, online and on your phone. Collab wishlists, groups and gift exchanges with other people on them are handed over to someone you pick. Wishes you added to someone else's collab wishlist stay there, without your name.
  • Without the app (you only used these web pages): e-mail us at the address below with the link of the list or group, and we delete what is yours.
