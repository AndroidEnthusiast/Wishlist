# Plan: forms, menus, settings and themes (owner's discussion, 17 November 2026)

Status: **waiting for the owner's approval. Nothing below is built yet.**
Inspiration: screenshots of GoWish (settings, wishlist menu, create form) and Giftful (themes). We make **our own**
version of each idea, never a copy of their look, icons or names.

Who it is for: everyone using the app. What gets better: the app feels calm, finished and recognisably its own,
instead of a set of small Android pop-ups.

Each step is its own update, with checks and screenshots (light and dark, Quiet and Gen Z chaos), in this order:
small and safe first, the big server change last.

## Step 1. Chips: text centred top to bottom (small)
- Every chip in the app (Love it / Private, occasion chips, Me/With others, filters): the text sits exactly in the
  middle between the top and bottom edge. The tick on a chosen chip stays.
- How: one shared chip shape with even padding and the font's extra space above and below the letters trimmed.
- Check: a test measures the gap above and below the text in each chip.

## Step 2. "New wishlist": My own / With others (small)
- Chips: **My own** and **With others**. Under "With others": "Invite people to add ideas with you."
- The name field ("Who are the gifts for?") becomes **optional**: useful for "Gifts for Mum" (the share screen then
  warns, Invite Friends leaves Mum out), not needed for a couple's own list.
- The word "Someone else" goes everywhere ("Wishlists for someone else" becomes "Shared wishlists").

## Step 3. One full-width form sheet (medium)
A new part of our own, used everywhere a form or a list menu opens:
- Slides up from the bottom, full width, fixed height (switching My own / With others never changes its size).
- Title centred, a round **X** top right, no Cancel button. The main button (Create, Save) stays at the bottom.
- Swipe down or X: slides down and closes at once, no question (owner).
- Used for: **New wishlist** (both kinds), **New wishlist group**, **New gift exchange**, **Wishlist settings**
  (name, description, occasion, visibility) and **a list's ⋮ menu**.
- **The list menu**: the list's own collage and name at the top with "3 wishes", then rows with an icon in a soft
  rounded tile (Share, Edit, Visibility, Reorder…), then Delete in red at the bottom. Replaces the small pop-up.
- Risk: a half-typed form is lost on a swipe (owner chose that). Gen Z chaos gets the same sheet in its own style.

## Step 4. Settings screen instead of the profile pop-up (medium)
Tapping your photo top right opens a full screen (no hidden items):
- A header card: photo, name, your About me line, and **Share** (your friend code).
- Two-column tiles: **Reserved gifts**, **Received gifts**, **Notifications**, **Bin**.
- Rows with ›: **Theme**, **App settings**, **Backup and recovery key**, **About** (licences, version).
- The version number at the bottom. Nothing removed, only grouped; every item that is in the pop-up today stays
  reachable (a check lists them).

## Step 5. Themes: five, plus Gen Z chaos as a bonus (large)
- **Five themes**, each tinting the whole app gently (page, cards, bottom bar, top buttons, the Wishlist wordmark,
  empty-photo gift emblem), hand-tuned so every text reads at 4.5 : 1 in **light and dark**. Proposed names and
  colours (owner to approve): **Berry** (default), **Ocean** (blue), **Forest** (green), **Amber** (warm orange),
  **Lilac** (violet). Sixth: **Gen Z chaos** (unchanged, saved in `themes/gen-z-chaos/`).
- **The colour wheel goes.** People with another colour get the closest theme automatically, once.
- **Theme screen** (our own layout, no app-icon picker): two-column cards, each a small preview of a wishlist card
  in that theme's colours, a tick on the chosen one; below it **Appearance**: System / Light / Dark as one segmented
  control.
- Risks: every screen changes colour, so screenshots of all main screens in all 6 themes, light and dark, before
  pushing; the web pages keep their current look (a later step if wanted).

## Step 6. Shared wishlists with hidden reservations: LEFT FOR NOW (owner, 17 November 2026)
Not built; kept here for later.

- A "With others" list gets **Hide reservations**, **on by default**: the collaborators (everyone invited to edit,
  you included) never see what is reserved, like your own list. Gifts are reserved by friends through the list's
  **friends' link** (brought back for shared lists, it was dropped in October 2026).
- Off: works like today (a group buying for Mum sees what's taken, so nobody buys it twice).
- The server must enforce it (never trust the app): with hiding on, the edit key can't read reservations.
- Risks to settle before building: old app versions of collaborators (they would get no reservations and must show
  a clear message, not an error), the browser editor (`edit.js`) and the friends' page, lists made before (they keep
  today's behaviour: hiding off), price alerts and "your reserved gift was moved to the bin" for hidden lists.

## Making the app feel like its own
- The theme colour carries through everything (the wordmark, bars, emblems), never just the buttons.
- Each list's own collage at the top of its menu and settings: you always see what you're working on.
- One signature shape: the rounded squircle for tiles, icon backgrounds and cards (already in `Squircle.kt`).
- Sheets slide with a soft spring; small haptic ticks on Reserve and Love it.

## Not in this plan
The web pages' look, the app icon, a wish's own page (still planned separately).
