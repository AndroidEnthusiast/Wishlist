Quiet, polished

The design plan for Quiet, step 3 of how design styles are made: the rules, then a mock-up of every key screen. Switch any screen between light and dark with the buttons below. Nothing is built until you approve it.

The wish leadsPhoto, name and price are the first things you see. Buttons step back: smaller, lighter, still 48 dp to tap.
One colour, used sparinglyYour accent marks the one main action and what is chosen. Everything else is ink and greys. No more black buttons.
Type makes the orderFraunces for titles, Figtree for the rest, five sizes with clear steps. Nothing smaller than 12.
Calm surfacesWhite cards on a soft grey page, dark grey on black. One photo surface, no frame in a frame. Shadows only on things that float.
Precise spacing16 between the screen edge and cards, 12 between cards, 28 between sections. Corners: 20 cards, 14 fields, round buttons.
Light and dark, both designedDark is true black with lifted greys, softer accent and its own price colours; both measured for contrast.
Changes every mock-up on this page. The accent is Indigo, the default.
